The Seven Steps

In a Hindu wedding the vows are not spoken standing still. Chaitali and Devraj will walk seven steps around the sacred fire, and each step carries a promise.

1

Food

अन्नम्

We walk the first step together and promise to keep a home where no one — family, friend or stranger — goes hungry.

2

Strength

बलम्

The second step is for strength: to grow strong in body and mind, and to be the one the other leans on.

3

Prosperity

धनम्

With the third step we ask for prosperity earned honestly, and the wisdom to share it generously.

4

Happiness

सुखम्

The fourth step is for happiness — to stay kind, laugh often, and look after each other in hard weeks as well as easy ones.

5

Family

सन्ततिः

The fifth step is a prayer for a loving family and for the blessings of the elders who raised us.

6

Health

ऋतवः

The sixth step asks for good health through every season of life, and years enough to see them all.

7

Friendship

मैत्री

With the seventh step we become lifelong companions. Sakha saptapadi bhava — after seven steps, we are friends for life.
